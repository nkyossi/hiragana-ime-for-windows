The language model of the LLM-assisted conversion of Hiragana IME for Windows

model.onnx  tohoku-nlp/bert-base-japanese-v3 (AutoModelForMaskedLM) exported to
            ONNX with tools/export-llm-model.py (opset 17, float32)
vocab.txt   its WordPiece vocabulary

The model was made by the Tohoku University NLP Group
(https://huggingface.co/tohoku-nlp/bert-base-japanese-v3) and is distributed
under the Apache License 2.0. It has only been converted to ONNX; the weights
are not changed.
